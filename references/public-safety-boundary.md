# Public Safety Boundary

Use this package for public demonstration and concept/coordination-grade research.

Do not add customer, employee, supplier, or organization identifiers; real project numbers; local paths; private URLs; credentials; unlicensed drawings or scans; proprietary standards; confidential geometry; equipment profiles; commercial statistics; or fabrication/NC logic.

Use synthetic or explicitly redistributable examples. Replace identifiers consistently, remove metadata, and test whether combined artifacts could re-identify or reconstruct a protected project. Do not call transformed real data synthetic.

Reconstruction does not establish structural adequacy. Do not infer load capacity, safety, regulatory compliance, or fitness for use from geometric agreement. Require appropriately authorized engineering review for consequential use.

Before release, scan content and archive metadata, then inspect every image, PDF, video, and slide manually for title blocks, logos, faces, signatures, location clues, or embedded filenames.
